How I work

I'm not a full-time consultant. I hold a senior in-house role and take on a small number of advisory engagements alongside it, fitting work around evenings and weekends. That keeps the day job sharp and means I only take on engagements I can genuinely deliver on.

Most work comes through referral — from people I've worked with, advised, or who know my work. If you've landed here directly, please mention who pointed you my way.

  • Availability: Outside of standard business hours (Mon–Fri 9–5). Async by default; calls scheduled evenings or weekends.
  • Scope: Strategic advisory, short engagements, and bounded project work. Not for full-time or fractional-executive arrangements.
  • Non-compete: I won't take engagements that overlap with my employer's sectors (beauty, tattoo, pet grooming supply, or otherwise conflict with Nexus Brands Group). Anything else is fair game.
  • Confidentiality: Happy to sign NDAs. I don't discuss client work publicly.
What I help with

All of this is grounded in what I've actually built — not theory. At Nexus Brands Group I designed and built the data function from scratch across 18 businesses: warehouse, BI, analytics, AI adoption, and the team to run it. The areas below are where I can compress years of trial-and-error into weeks of advice for someone else doing the same.

  • Data system design from scratch: end-to-end architecture for companies with no data function yet — what to build, in what order, what to skip. Hourly syncs from NetSuite, Shopify, Google, etc. into a centralised warehouse using Python, SQL, Polars, DuckDB, and Docker.
  • BI & reporting from zero: replacing legacy spreadsheets and disconnected tools with a unified Streamlit (or equivalent) stack — SSO, role-based access, 20+ dashboards covering exec, ops, finance, marketing. Pragmatic alternative to per-seat BI licences.
  • Analytics & commercial modelling: CAC modelling and optimisation, pricing & shipping margin analysis with statistical significance testing, basket & bundle intelligence, discount dependency, vendor spend, inventory and OTIF diagnostics, EOS L10 scorecards.
  • AI enablement: embedding LLM-assisted development (Claude Code) into data and engineering teams to compress pipeline and dashboard delivery. Helping leaders separate hype from where AI actually moves the needle.
  • Self-hosted local LLMs: standing up private, zero-marginal-cost models for sensitive workloads — review sentiment, SEO copy analysis, product description harmonisation, classification — with prompts tuned per task. No data leaves your network.
  • Hiring & team build: what a first data hire should look like, when to add the second, how to structure a small high-leverage team. Drawn from building and leading a team of PhD-level scientists and engineers.
  • Technical due diligence: independent reviews of data & AI stacks for investors, acquirers, or boards considering an investment.
