SIGNAL BRIEF

See your music. Nyquist is a real-time audio spectrum analyzer for your Mac, built with Metal, up to 128 frequency bands.

Capture system audio from any app — Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube, games — or switch to your microphone for live input.

CAPABILITIES

8192-POINT FFT

High-resolution frequency analysis with sub-bin interpolation for precise low-end detail.

SYSTEM AUDIO

Captures anything playing on your Mac. System audio or microphone.

METAL RENDERED

GPU-accelerated rendering. Minimal CPU usage. Apple Silicon and Intel.

NOW PLAYING

See track info from Apple Music and Spotify. Play, pause, skip — without leaving the visualizer.

MINI PLAYER

Always-on-top strip you can drag anywhere. Shows now playing.

DISPLAY MODES

CUSTOMIZABLE

BANDS 16 — 128 adjustable
MODES Normal + Mirror
COLORS Orange, Green, Amber, Cyan, White
INPUT System Audio + Microphone
GAIN 0.1x — 5.0x
WINDOW Pin, Mini
PRIVACY

Nyquist processes all audio locally on your device. No data is collected, stored, or transmitted. Audio capture requires your permission and can be revoked at any time in System Settings.
